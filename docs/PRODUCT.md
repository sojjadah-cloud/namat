# NAMAT Product Documentation

## Vision
NAMAT is a Personalized Health, Wellness & Lifestyle Ecosystem.
"Choose your goal. NAMAT connects the services, professionals, activities, meals, products and challenges that help you build a healthier lifestyle."
Philosophy: Goal → Journey → Services → Action → Progress → Motivation.

## Personas & User Roles
1. **User**: The core consumer looking to improve their lifestyle, book consultations, order healthy meals, join challenges, and track progress.
2. **Family Member**: A linked account under a primary household profile.
3. **Specialist**: Professional consultants offering nutrition, fitness, or medical guidance.
4. **Partner (Restaurant/Sport/Shop)**: Service providers managing their offerings, menus, schedules, and inventory.
5. **Admin**: Platform administrators overseeing the ecosystem, approving partners, and managing users.

## Core Modules & Journeys
1. **My Journey**: The central hub connecting the user's goals, progress, and upcoming activities.
2. **Consult**: Specialist discovery, profile viewing, and consultation booking (video/in-person).
3. **Meals**: Healthy food discovery, restaurant menus, dietary filters, cart, and checkout.
4. **Sport**: Discovery of gyms, classes, outdoor activities, maps, and activity booking.
5. **Shop**: E-commerce for fitness gear, nutrition products, and wellness accessories.
6. **Challenges**: Personal, friend-vs-friend, group, and community challenges.
7. **Packages**: Curated lifestyle bundles (e.g., Starter, Weight Management, Athlete).
8. **Partner Portal**: Dashboard for partners to manage their business on NAMAT.
9. **Admin Portal**: Secure platform management.

## Business Rules & Principles
* **No Medical Diagnoses**: NAMAT connects users to professionals but does not diagnose.
* **Privacy**: Sensitive wellness data is access-controlled and not shared casually.
* **No Dead Buttons**: Every UI element must be functional or intentionally disabled with explanation.
* **Localization**: The platform supports English (LTR) and Arabic (RTL).
